Aesthetic: Wishcore — https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Wishcore

How the CSS expresses it:

Wishcore's key colors are pastel pink, light blue, cream, and subtle shimmer. On top of the palette swap I lots of soft gradients, rounded corners to show how wishcore is meant to be soft. As well as stars everywhere representing the wichu which is NCT Wish's maskot. I also have wichu itself as an image flying around the page.

-----------------

JavaScript enhancement

A "make a wish" button reveals an aside with a soft, inside you find NCT Wish member Sakuya singing the lyrics to "Make a Wish" by NCT U. As well as reveaing two music videos. One is a cover of "Make a Wish" performed by NCT Wish and the other is Poppop NCT Wish's own song that has an aeesthetic that represents wishcore really well.

-----------------

A usability choice

The make a wish button is only shown when JS is enabled. Additionally for accessibility, the animations will not play if the user has requested reduced motion in their system preferences. So all those stars and wichu will not be animated if the user has requested reduced motion. 

-----------------

Medium is the message

The words on this page are identical to the default About Me page what changes is the aesthetic. So it follows what McLuhan’s idea that the medium is the message. The aesthetic here is changing the way the user experiences the page and the content. But the content in words remains the same.
